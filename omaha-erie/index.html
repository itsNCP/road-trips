Worth knowing before you leaveLate July on the Plains means real afternoon thunderstorm risk along this whole corridor — this week specifically has had an active severe pattern (hail, high winds, isolated tornadoes flagged for parts of western Nebraska/northeast Colorado). It's usually a manageable summer pattern, not a reason to change plans, but check the NWS forecast the morning you leave rather than relying on this. If a warning is ever issued while you're driving, get off the highway and into a sturdy building rather than trying to outdrive it.

Stretch + photoGreat Platte River Road Archway

A museum literally built as an arch spanning I-80 at Exit 275 — walk across the highway inside it. Oregon Trail and Lincoln Highway history exhibits, plus a hike/bike trail and picnic grounds if you just want to stretch your legs without paying admission.

$12 adults / $11 seniors / $6 kids 6-13, open daily 9am-5pm (Sun noon-5pm).

Read this parked, not while driving Only the "safe while moving" cards below are meant for behind the wheel — everything else is for red lights or actual stops. Pair breaks with your charging stops; that rhythm is already built in.
Setup Seat, once before you leave ›

Hips level with or just above knees

Raise seat height until you look slightly down at the wheel. Knees bent 20–30° — never locked straight, that strains the joint.

Tailbone all the way back

Scoot flush into the seat crease before adjusting anything else. No gap between low back and seat back.

Recline to 100–110°

Not bolt upright — 90° actually loads the discs more than a slight recline does.

Lumbar support at belt line

Fill the low-back curve with built-in support or a rolled towel. No pressure point, no gap.

Wheel 10–12" from chest, hands 9 & 3

Elbows softly bent. Set mirrors after the seat — if you have to lean to see them, posture already slipped.

Knee note: keep a two-finger gap between the front of the seat and the back of your knee, so the cushion isn't pressing into the knee pit on a long drive.

Hip flexor stretch, door-assisted

Hold the open car door, step one foot back into a soft standing lunge. Back knee gently bent, not locked — tuck the tailbone under to feel it in the front hip.

Hold 20–30 sec each side

Seated figure-4

Sit on the tailgate or seat edge, cross one ankle over the opposite knee, hinge forward slightly. Easier on arthritic knees than the standing version.

Hold 20–30 sec each side

Hamstring stretch, heel on sill

Heel on the door sill or running board, knee soft — not locked — hinge forward from the hips with a flat back.

Hold 20–30 sec each side

Walk it out

Flat, grassy ground if you can find it — the single best reset for hips, low back, and a restless mind. Pair with the dog's walk.

5–10 min, every stop

Calf raises

Hand on the car for balance, rise onto the balls of both feet, lower slowly. Pumps blood through stiff feet and ankles without pounding the joints.

10–12 slow reps
Arthritis rule: never force a stretch to a locked-straight knee or a deep bent-knee end range. Mild pulling is fine; sharp, pinching, or swelling-triggering pain means back off and try the seated version.
Massage Self-massage, no gear ›

Glute release against the car

Back up to the car, make a fist, lean your weight into the fleshy part of one glute. Small circles or slow rocking.

30–45 sec each side

Low-back release, standing

Two soft fists either side of the spine, just above the hip bones — never on the spine itself. Lean back and let your weight sink in, small figure-8s.

30–60 sec

Seated hip / piriformis dig

Seat edge, figure-4 position, thumb or knuckles into the meaty part of the glute near the hip crease — often the real source of "sciatica" tightness from driving.

45 sec each side

Foot knead, seated
